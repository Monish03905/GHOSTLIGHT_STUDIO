        How the network grows itself

        1. I
          It publishes like a channel, not a dump. Fixed airtimes per identity, a warm backlog film ready, and one upload at a time so the feed never looks automated.
        2. II
          Every film ships with its own reach. Captions, thumbnail, playlist, hashtags, a share kit and an optional Discord or RSS announcement go out with the upload.
        3. III
          It reads the room. Analytics become a playbook — which niches travel, which registers hold attention, which hour earns subscribers — and the next film is chosen from that.
        4. IV
          It keeps exploring. A deliberate slice of every decision is random, so the network never collapses into one repeated formula. Novelty is enforced before a film is ever written.

        Growth is earned film by film. The studio cannot buy viewers, and revenue depends on watch time, region and YouTube's programme rules — the ledger below is what actually happened, not a forecast.
